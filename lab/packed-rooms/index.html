One virtual show in 2020; live in Toronto, Calgary and Montreal since, with Toronto 2026 announced.

  1. 2020

    One virtual show

    The Allstate Soccer Show began with one virtual event created during the pandemic to keep fans connected.

  2. 2021

  3. 2022

    Toronto
