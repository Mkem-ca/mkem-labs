The Sports Card & Memorabilia Expo

MKEM moved the Sport Card & Memorabilia Expo online with eBay and Hopin when COVID-19 shut down live events, then returned it to the floor in November 2021 and expanded it beyond Toronto.

2020When COVID-19 shut down live events, mkem led the charge in transitioning the Expo online, producing three highly successful virtual shows that kept collectors, dealers, and fans connected.

2021The Expo returned in person, breaking all previous records for both attendance and exhibitors. Since then it has expanded beyond Toronto with new editions in Montreal (twice a year), Edmonton, Quebec City, and Strongsville, Ohio.
