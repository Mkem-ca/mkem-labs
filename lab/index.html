mkem.ca rebuild · homepage direction studies

Seven ways the new homepage could feel

Each study builds one direction all the way in its opening moment and one signature section, then fills the rest of the page with shared pieces. They are explorations, not finished pages: the point is to choose a direction.

  1. 01Packed Rooms

    reviewed · approved

    An expo floor plan draws itself, a GPU crowd streams in through five doors and fills its aisles, and the drawing’s callout window fills with the real Sport Card Expo floor. The crowd stays on the page and sorts itself into the numbers.

    Look at first

    • Watch the first six seconds, then move the pointer through the crowd: it parts like an aisle.
    • The callout window the photograph lands in is part of the plan, and the crowd stands in the aisles around it.
    • Scroll slowly to the stats and hover one to scatter it, then the doorway at the end.
    Open Packed Rooms →

  2. 02Collector’s Edition

    reviewed · approved

    The Sport Card Expo heritage made premium. A sealed pack rips into a fanned hand of foil cards, case studies are graded slabs, the team are trading cards.

    Look at first

    • Wait four seconds for the pack to rip itself, or drag it. Then move the pointer across the hand: the foil is a real thin-film shader.
    • The shelf wall and the certification slabs.
    • Flip a team card.
    Open Collector’s Edition →

  3. 03Render to Reality

    reviewed · approved

    Watch an event get built. A blueprint of the HIROC ballroom becomes assembling blocks, then the photograph, with drawing callouts carrying the real facts.

    Look at first

    • The five-second build on load, then drag the handle to scrub concept, build, show.
    • Sheet 04: four services that build as they enter.
    • Hover a case card to resolve it.
    Open Render to Reality →

  4. 04Game Day

    reviewed · approved

    A title screen. The page opens inches from an LED wall, pulls back to the real stage, and wears an 8-bit HUD: a countdown to the next show, a scoreboard, a keyboard level select, and pixel characters who walk the venue.

    Look at first

    • The pull-back on load, then hover the wall.
    • Level select: use the left and right arrow keys.
    • The characters along the sections and the team cards. Try the Konami code on the sponsor strip.
    Open Game Day →

  5. 05All-Access Pass

    approved · drag the badge · logo pin question

    You are an attendee. A 3D lanyard badge drops in and hangs on a real strap, and walking the venue snaps pixel pins onto it. All seven turns the security strip gold and the pass prints at the exit.

    Look at first

    • The badge dropping in, swinging once, and the security strip catching the light. Then pick it up: it follows the cursor exactly, the strap goes taut, and letting go swings it back.
    • Scroll into a zone and watch its pin fly from the signage and stamp onto the badge, which has shrunk into the corner.
    • The exit: the badge flips to its record, the strip goes gold, and the pass feeds out of the printer.
    Open All-Access Pass →

  6. 06Midway

    reviewed · approved

    A small isometric pixel expo hall you drift across, every landmark real work, characters walking the aisles. Scrolling is the camera moving down the floor, from the entrance to the ticket booth.

    Look at first

    • The first few seconds: the crowd walking the aisles, the queue at registration, footage on the stage screens.
    • Scroll the whole hall: sponsor towers, the board over the stage rolling its numbers up, four district banners, four landmark booths, the show office, the ticket booth.
    • Hover a landmark booth to light it and name the client, then open it: the case footage moves onto the booth’s own screen and onto the screens over the main stage.
    Open Midway →

  7. 07Show Control

    reviewed · approved · wording pending founder

    The homepage as the production desk. A pinned control panel on the left runs a cue list, timecode and meters; the show on the right answers each cue as you scroll.

    Look at first

    • The first five seconds: three cues fire on the desk, the house comes up, the Expo walk-in rolls, the headline sets.
    • Scroll and watch the cue list: every section lights its cue green and the desk stamps the time it went.
    • Click a cue to jump to it, or press the space bar to fire the next one. The “How we run a show” panel sits on the desk.
    Open Show Control →
