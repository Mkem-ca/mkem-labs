Sport Card & Memorabilia Expo2020 →

The Sports Card & Memorabilia Expo

MKEM moved the Sport Card & Memorabilia Expo online with eBay and Hopin when COVID-19 shut down live events, then returned it to the floor in November 2021 and expanded it beyond Toronto.

3Virtual shows produced with eBay and Hopin · mkem.ca homepage, Client highlights
Record attendance and exhibitorsIn-person return, November 2021 · mkem.ca homepage, Client highlights
2nd largestSport card event in the world, behind only The National in Chicago · mkem.ca homepage, Client highlights
  1. 2020

    Three virtual shows with eBay and Hopin

    When COVID-19 shut down live events, mkem led the charge in transitioning the Expo online, producing three highly successful virtual shows that kept collectors, dealers, and fans connected.

  2. 2021

    In-person return, November

    The Expo returned in person, breaking all previous records for both attendance and exhibitors. Since then it has expanded beyond Toronto with new editions in Montreal (twice a year), Edmonton, Quebec City, and Strongsville, Ohio.
